# Lead-story rubric

Grade every headline for one question: **how good a lead would this be for
Weary's edition that day?** Weary covers the single most important AI story of
the day. A story is in scope only when AI is central to it: AI models,
products, companies, chips and compute, research, AI policy and law, or AI
used in an attack. A security incident, crime, court case or outage with no AI
angle is off topic, however serious.

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 3 | The best lead that day: the most consequential event in the list. Usually one item, at most two if they report the same event. |
| 2 | A strong alternative: clearly consequential, a reasonable editor could lead with it. |
| 1 | In scope but minor: a real AI story with limited consequence (a routine product update, a small study). |
| 0 | Not a lead: off topic (including security and other technology news with no AI angle), promotional, or a how-to or tutorial. |

Weigh concrete public consequence (people affected, money, law, safety) and
how reliable the source is. Ignore popularity and promotional volume. Two
headlines about the same event get the same grade. Grade each headline on its
own merits: the grades are not a ranking and several items may share a grade.
