Anthropic said it closed an initial public offering of 1,127,000,000 shares of Class A common stock at $83.00 a share, including the full exercise of underwriters’ option to buy 147,000,000 additional shares. The company said 1,103,351,354 shares were sold by Anthropic and 23,648,646 by selling stockholders, with about $93.5 billion in gross proceeds before expenses and about $91.6 billion received by Anthropic. Anthropic said its Class A shares began trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on Nov. 19, 2026, under the symbol ANT.

Anthropic also said it closed a concurrent private placement of 313,253,007 shares of Class N common stock to Advanced Micro Devices, Broadcom, Google, Micron Technology, Microsoft, NVIDIA and TSMC Partners at the same $83.00 offering price. The company said that sale brought in about $26.0 billion and was not registered under the Securities Act. Taken together, Anthropic said the IPO and private placement produced about $119.5 billion in gross proceeds before underwriting discounts, commissions and other offering expenses.
