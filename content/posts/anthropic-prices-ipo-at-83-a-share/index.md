Anthropic priced its initial public offering of Class A common stock at $83 a share, according to a company statement. The offering covers 980 million shares, with 956,351,354 sold by Anthropic and 23,648,646 sold by certain existing stockholders. Anthropic said it will not receive proceeds from shares sold by those stockholders.

The company said the shares are expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on Nov. 19, 2026, under the symbol “ANT.” It said the offering is expected to close on Nov. 20, 2026, subject to customary closing conditions, and that underwriters have a 30-day option to buy up to 147 million additional shares.

Anthropic said a registration statement for the securities was declared effective by the Securities and Exchange Commission on Nov. 18, 2026, and that the offering is being made only by means of a prospectus.
