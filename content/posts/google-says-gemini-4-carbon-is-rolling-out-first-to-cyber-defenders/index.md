Google said on Oct. 28 that Gemini 4 Carbon is its “next frontier model” and that it is starting access with a subset of cyber defenders before widening availability. The company said the model will later reach developers, businesses and consumers after further feedback and guardrail work.

In the company’s description, Carbon is intended to improve software engineering and cybersecurity defense, and Google said it is engaging in the U.S. government’s voluntary pre-release testing process. Google also said Carbon follows Gemini 4 Argon, which it launched two weeks earlier.

Google said Carbon ranks first on an updated Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and that it posted gains on coding and agentic tasks, as well as on cybersecurity benchmarks for vulnerability finding, exploitation and patching. The company also said internal testing showed stronger secure-code generation and lower misalignment and fabrication rates in live coding settings.
