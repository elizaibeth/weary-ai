The White House on Tuesday announced America.gov, a streamlined front door intended to simplify how citizens access federal services online. Google is named as a technology partner in the effort, with Gemini described as the tool to help more than 100 million people reach critical public resources with greater speed and ease.

In a company statement, the partnership is framed as supporting the administration’s digital modernization goals and making everyday public services seamless, accessible, and responsive for Americans.

The announcement characterizes the initiative as part of digital modernization for public services, with Google leveraging Gemini to reach a large user base.
