Nvidia said artificial intelligence is helping semiconductor manufacturers shorten some of the slowest parts of chip development, including simulations used to study plasma behavior in etch processes. The company said Lam Research showed that NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo could predict plasma behavior in milliseconds, compared with days for a full simulation.

In the company’s account, the point is not to replace engineers but to reduce the time they spend waiting for answers. Nvidia said AI and digital twins can help teams optimize processes, spot problems earlier and test changes virtually before physical trials, while engineers still make the decisions.

The company framed this as part of what it called an AI-defined fab, where accelerated computing shortens simulation time and lets engineers examine more alternatives before committing to experiments on the production line.
