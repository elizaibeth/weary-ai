Anthropic, PBC said it filed a registration statement with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission for a proposed initial public offering of its Class A common stock. The company said it intends to list the shares on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol ANT, but has not yet set the number of shares or the price range.

The filing said the proposed offering would be made only through a prospectus and that the registration statement has not yet become effective. It also said the securities may not be sold, and offers to buy may not be accepted, until the filing is effective.
