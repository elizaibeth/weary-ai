Anthropic said it launched the roadshow for its proposed initial public offering of Class A common stock. The company said the offering will total 825,000,000 shares, with 801,351,354 shares sold by Anthropic and 23,648,646 shares sold by existing stockholders. Anthropic said it would not receive proceeds from shares sold by the stockholders, and said underwriters are expected to have a 30-day option to buy up to 123,750,000 additional shares.

The company said the offering price is expected to be between $70 and $78 a share and that it has applied to list the stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol ANT. Anthropic said the sale will be made only through a prospectus, and that the securities cannot be sold until the registration statement becomes effective.
