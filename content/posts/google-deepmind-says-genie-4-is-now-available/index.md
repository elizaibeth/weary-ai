Google DeepMind said Genie 4 is now available, introducing the latest version of its world model on Nov. 18, 2026. The company said Genie 3 remains in Project Genie’s model picker for longer runs or panorama-seeded spaces.

The company said Genie 4 lets users change a created world while it is running by entering new prompts during a session. It said the model can shift weather, add characters or redirect action in real time, and that it also adds native audio that changes with the scene, including ambient sound and dialogue.

Google DeepMind also said Project Genie now includes an agent that can take a plain-language description and assemble an interactive world from it. The company said the system can use multimodal models to create initial visuals and background music, and can bring Gemini models into a session to suggest next steps or trigger events.

In a separate post, the company said Genie 4 can be steered in real time with text captions from Gemini and used with Gemini 4 Argon in simulations that adapt while they are being rendered. It described the setup as a way to simulate scenarios in imagined worlds and said one example involved a humanoid playing checkers.
