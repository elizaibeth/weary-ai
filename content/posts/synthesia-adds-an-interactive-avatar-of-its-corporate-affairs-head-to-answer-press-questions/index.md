Synthesia announced the addition in a blog post titled "Say Hello to Synthesia's New AI Press Officer," dated August 26, 2026 and written by Alexandru Voica, who is identified in the byline as head of corporate affairs and policy at Synthesia. In the post, Synthesia said it was introducing an AI press officer to its Newsroom: an Interactive Avatar built with the voice and likeness of Voica, its global head of corporate affairs, that answers questions about Synthesia in a live conversation, drawing on information about the company provided by a large language model.

The press officer runs on Synthesia's Interactive Avatars technology, which the company says lets an avatar listen, talk and respond as a person speaks. Synthesia renders the avatar, including its speech and expressions, while the conversation draws on company information provided by a large language model trained to retain Synthesia-related facts and figures.

In a blog post introducing the AI press officer, Synthesia says the avatar is designed to answer a range of general questions about the company: what it does, how the platform works, its approach to responsible AI, and how customers use its products. For anything more specific, including media requests, interview scheduling, and questions about particular announcements, the post directs people to email press@synthesia.io, where a member of the team will get back to them.

In the Synthesia blog post "Say Hello to Synthesia's New AI Press Officer" (August 26, 2026), Alexandru Voica wrote that "The press officer is itself an example of how we build and ship innovative experiences quickly and transparently."

On the announcement page, the 'You might also like' section links to related posts, including a post about Synthesia building the global AI video platform for business and expanding in the U.S.; an Interactive Avatar API post described as embedding a real-time, lip-synced avatar into your product using your own LLM, STT and TTS, available now for Enterprise customers; a post announcing Roleplay Sessions for interactive coaching and practice with avatars; and a post announcing the new AI Assistant for faster video creation and the Express-3 avatar model, Synthesia's most lifelike avatar model yet. The page also lists Synthesia as SOC 2 Type II compliant, ISO 42001 compliant, and GDPR compliant.

Synthesia Ltd is based in London. A Synthesia blog post titled "Building the Global AI Video Platform for Business: Why Synthesia is Expanding in the U.S.," by Laura Gonzalez and dated April 21, 2026, announced that the company has opened its new US headquarters in New York City: 50,000 square feet in Manhattan's Flatiron District, nearly four times the size of its previous space. The post added that around half of Synthesia's revenue now comes from the US.
