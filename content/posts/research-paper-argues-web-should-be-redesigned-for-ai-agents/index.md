A research paper posted June 1 argues that websites should be redesigned for AI agents rather than forcing agents to work around human-built interfaces. The paper proposes an Agentic Web Interface, or AWI, as a web design approach intended specifically for agents to navigate websites.

The authors say current web agents face problems because human-designed interfaces do not fit LLM capabilities. They say existing methods struggle with large DOM trees, screenshot-based inputs and API-only interactions, and they outline six guiding principles for AWI design centered on safety, efficiency and standardization.
