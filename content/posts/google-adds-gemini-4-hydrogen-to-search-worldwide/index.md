Google said on Nov. 12 that it is adding Gemini 4 Hydrogen to Search worldwide for all users, describing it as the largest model upgrade to Search so far. The company said the update is meant to produce more helpful, visual and intuitive responses, and to improve how Search handles complex or multi-part questions.

In a separate post on Gemini models, Google said Hydrogen is built for fast execution across coding and agentic workflows and is being made available across Google AI Studio, the Gemini API, Gemini for business, the Gemini app and Google Search. The company also said the model is available in Google Sheets for Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.

Google said Hydrogen is intended to follow instructions more precisely, including requests with typos or multiple parts, and to support richer language understanding across all supported AI Mode languages. The company also said the model pulls context from uploads and Google apps such as Gmail and Google Drive, along with connected apps including Airtable and Dropbox.
