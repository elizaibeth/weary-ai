Microsoft said its Microsoft 365 consumer services were unavailable for some users, and OpenAI and Anthropic separately reported elevated errors affecting their Microsoft 365 connector. Microsoft later said the services had fully recovered and the backlog of email messages had been processed, while OpenAI and Anthropic each said the connector problems had been resolved.

Microsoft said the issue began Thursday, Nov. 12, 2026, at 10:05 a.m. UTC and ended Friday, Nov. 13, 2026, at 4:10 p.m. UTC. OpenAI said its Microsoft 365 connector was operating normally again in an update posted Nov. 14, and Anthropic said on Nov. 13 at 21:49 UTC that its incident had been resolved. Anthropic also said mail, calendar and search tools in the Microsoft 365 connector and Claude for Outlook returned errors for many requests from Nov. 12 at 16:03 UTC to Nov. 13 at 16:40 UTC because of a Microsoft 365 service incident.

The reports show a Microsoft service outage reaching into AI products that connect to Microsoft 365, with each company describing its own user-facing errors and recovery. Microsoft said the backlog of email messages had been processed, OpenAI said affected services were operating normally again, and Anthropic said its connector and Claude for Outlook had been resolved after many requests returned errors.
